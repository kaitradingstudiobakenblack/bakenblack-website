Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Bake n Black is not specifically directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact
